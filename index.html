About

In the School of Mary: A Student’s Guide to the Mysteries of the Rosary.

Built by Claude using material provided by and under the guidance of Keith Francis.

Keith set the scope, audience and purpose of the guide. Claude researched, drafted and typeset the text and built this app and the companion booklet.

As with any study aid, check its notes against Scripture, the Catechism and the teaching of the Church, and take questions to a priest or catechist.

Licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Scripture references follow the RSV-2CE; the traditional prayers are in the public domain.
